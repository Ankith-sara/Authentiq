Backend Framework
FastAPI (main.py)
The web server that exposes your API endpoints.
Chosen because it's async-native (important for non-blocking ML inference), auto-generates OpenAPI docs, and has clean Pydantic validation built in.
Pydantic
Validates incoming JSON payloads — enforces that text is between 10 and 50,000 characters before it even reaches your ML code.
SlowAPI
Rate limiting middleware.
Caps requests at 30/minute per IP globally, 20/minute on heavy endpoints, 10/minute on PDF upload.
Prevents abuse and protects model compute.
Uvicorn
The ASGI server that actually runs FastAPI.
Handles async I/O at the HTTP level.
AI Detection (ai_detector.py)
GPT-2 (transformers, torch)
Used purely as a perplexity calculator — not a classifier.
It measures how "surprising" each token is.
Lower perplexity = smoother, more predictable text = AI-like.
GPT-2 is used here because it's small, fast, and well understood — not because it's the best detector.
DeBERTa-v3-base (transformers)
The primary classifier.
A 2021-era Microsoft model with disentangled attention that understands context far better than GPT-2.
It's loaded as a binary sequence classifier (human vs AI).
If fine-tuned weights exist on disk, they're loaded automatically.
Heuristic signals (pure Python — statistics, re, collections)
Six hand-crafted features — burstiness, entropy, AI phrase density, structural uniformity, unique word ratio, sentence length.
No ML needed, fast to compute, and serve as the fallback when DeBERTa isn't trained yet.
scikit-learn (LogisticRegression, roc_curve)
Used only for Platt scaling calibration — fits a logistic regression to map raw scores to reliable probabilities, and reports ROC AUC before/after.
NumPy
Array operations for embeddings and score averaging in the paraphrase robustness layer.
Plagiarism Detection (plagiarism_engine.py)
SentenceTransformers (all-MiniLM-L6-v2)
Converts sentences into 384-dimensional semantic vectors.
all-MiniLM-L6-v2 is used specifically because it's tiny (80MB), fast, and very good at semantic similarity — the sweet spot for a local plagiarism checker.
FAISS (faiss-cpu)
Facebook's vector similarity library.
Builds an index of all corpus sentence embeddings and does nearest-neighbour search in milliseconds, even with thousands of stored sentences.
IndexFlatIP = exact inner-product (cosine) search.
sample_corpus.txt + submissions.txt
The "database." Every submission gets split into sentences and appended to submissions.txt.
On the next check, those sentences are in the index — so the system learns from everything it has ever seen.
SerpAPI (requests, optional)
If a SERPAPI_KEY env var is set, the top 3 most-similar sentences are also checked against live Google results.
Disabled by default.

Fifth, warmup duration emerges as a critical hyperparameter requiring careful configuration.	598f5eb4-c5ea-4bbf-aa4f-7cc89ca97bbf

Determining optimal

resource -constrained scenarios.	598f5eb4-c5ea-4bbf-aa4f-7cc89ca97bbf
The additional computation required for learnin g rate

Generalization across architectures and domains remains problematic.	598f5eb4-c5ea-4bbf-aa4f-7cc89ca97bbf

Scaling challenges emerge as model sizes continue growing.	598f5eb4-c5ea-4bbf-aa4f-7cc89ca97bbf

to models with hundreds of billions of parameters.	598f5eb4-c5ea-4bbf-aa4f-7cc89ca97bbf

Finally, evaluation methodology inconsistencies across studies complicate direct comparison.	598f5eb4-c5ea-4bbf-aa4f-7cc89ca97bbf

Analysis, Discussion, and Interpretation  
The evolution of adaptive learning rate scheduling for transformers reflects broader trends in

specific optimization.	598f5eb4-c5ea-4bbf-aa4f-7cc89ca97bbf
The persistent popularity of warmup -cosine scheduling despite emergence of more complex

Layer -wise learning rate differentiation represents a particularly promising direction, supported

However, determining optimal layer -wise rate config urations remains challenging, 
with most studies applying heuristic rules rather than principled optimization procedures.	598f5eb4-c5ea-4bbf-aa4f-7cc89ca97bbf
Development of automated methods for layer -wise rate determination could substantially 
advance practical applicability.	598f5eb4-c5ea-4bbf-aa4f-7cc89ca97bbf
The tension between schedule complexity and computational overhead highlights a 
fundamental tradeoff in optimization research.	598f5eb4-c5ea-4bbf-aa4f-7cc89ca97bbf
While sophisticated adaptive methods can 
reduce required training iterations, the computational cost of adaptation itself may neg ate 
these benefits for smaller models.	598f5eb4-c5ea-4bbf-aa4f-7cc89ca97bbf
Future research should explicitly quantify total 
computational cost, including scheduling overhead, rather than focusing exclusively on 
iteration counts or wall -clock training time.	598f5eb4-c5ea-4bbf-aa4f-7cc89ca97bbf
Meta -learning approaches for scheduler discovery represent an intellectually appealing

disparities in AI research capacity.	598f5eb4-c5ea-4bbf-aa4f-7cc89ca97bbf
